### Summary

The artifact is well-sourced (every claim tags correctly, `[scope]` is used only to describe process depth, and superseded answers like Q2→Q17 and Q16→Q20 are tracked cleanly with no stray unselected-option leakage), and the deliberate fee-model deviation from the original `FeeCycle` schema is recorded clearly enough for Domain Design to act on. But the Success Metrics section is currently decorative — three measures with zero numbers give Requirements Analysis nothing concrete to trace to, which is the kind of gap that should be closed before this gate rather than carried forward as routine. Combined with a privacy boundary that is implied but not explicitly stated for the cross-teacher case, and a silent drop of the account-deletion/Data Safety scope item from the original brief, this is implementable in most respects but not yet ready to sign off without the human either supplying rough numeric targets or explicitly accepting that gap as a named limitation.
