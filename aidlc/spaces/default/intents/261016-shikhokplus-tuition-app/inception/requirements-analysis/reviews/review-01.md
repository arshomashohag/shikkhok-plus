### Summary

This is a strong, disciplined artifact — the three Q14 overrides are faithfully carried through, the ID scheme is clean, and the accepted-risks section is honest rather than softened. But it is not ready: a Must Have capability from the fixed backlog (guardian child selection) has silently disappeared with no FR and no open-question entry, one NFR is genuinely untestable in a document that otherwise insists on testability, and one assumption (A6) smuggles in a product definition that should be a stated requirement or a question back to the human. These are fixable in a single pass, not a sign of a fundamentally broken artifact — but they are exactly the class of gap this stage exists to catch before Domain Design inherits them.
