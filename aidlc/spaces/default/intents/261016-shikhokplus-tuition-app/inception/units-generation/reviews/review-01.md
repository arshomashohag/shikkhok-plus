### Summary

The decomposition is sound in its broad strokes — the vertical-slice rationale, the service-kind argument, and most of the non-obvious edges (api-contract→money-core, teacher-insights' two edges, the US9.1 non-edge) all check out against the upstream artifacts I could verify. The blocking issue is the walking-skeleton standalone-runtime claim: U3's lens resolver depends, at the component level, on Ownership and Enrollment tables that are U4's to create, and nothing in either units-generation artifact accounts for how those tables exist when U3 runs its Construction Verification Command. That is a genuine missing-edge/missing-scope gap in the document that would cost real rework if a developer builds U3 literally as scoped. The Teaching-split-vs-ADR-008 silence is a real documentation gap but turned out, on inspection, to likely be a non-issue in substance (the concurrency mechanism is Session-scoped, not shared with Mark) — it is reported as Major because the decomposition should say so rather than leave the contradiction with an explicit ADR unaddressed.
