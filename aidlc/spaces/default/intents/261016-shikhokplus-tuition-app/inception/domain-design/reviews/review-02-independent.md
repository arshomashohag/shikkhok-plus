### Summary

The component boundaries, the acyclic dependency graph, and the privacy/concurrency mechanisms (ADR-001, ADR-006) hold up under independent re-derivation, and the already-fixed ledger invariant and Money-type annotations check out where they were applied. But two Major gaps block readiness. First, ADR-003 cites a per-table deletion-disposition manifest in `components.md` that does not exist there — only 2 of roughly 24 entities have a stated disposition anywhere in this artifact, which means Governance's core deliverable (the manifest a developer transcribes into code) is undecided for the large majority of tables, and the claim that "the privacy policy is now generatable" is premature. Second, the Money-type boundary — enforced structurally everywhere else in this design via explicit `depends_on: Money` declarations and per-field notes — silently excludes `Batch.default_fee` and `Enrollment.fee_amount`, two of the fee-anchor values the whole fee-cycle model is built around, leaving a plausible path for a `Float` or untyped `Numeric` column to enter the one place the project's permanent money rule most needs to hold. Both are fixable with additions to the existing artifact rather than redesign, but both are the kind of gap a developer would have to guess past rather than build from.
