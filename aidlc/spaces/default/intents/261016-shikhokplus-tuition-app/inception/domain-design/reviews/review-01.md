| R-01 | Major | aidlc/spaces/default/intents/261016-shikhokplus-tuition-app/inception/domain-design/decisions.md > ADR-002, "The invariant, corrected" | The corrected invariant is still unbalanced in exactly the overpayment/credit scenario it exists to fix. Trace it: cycle 1 charges 1000, a payment of 1200 is approved, recorded per the decision's own rule as `receipt(1000)` applied plus `credit_grant(200)` for the excess. At the enrollment level: `total_charged(1000) + total_carried(0) - total_received(1000) - total_forgiven(0) = 0`, but `total_outstanding(0) - available_credit(200) = -200`. `0 != -200` — the stated identity fails on its own worked example. ### Summary

The catalogue's structural claims hold up against the upstream requirements and against each other: the dependency graph is genuinely acyclic, the teacher-vs-teacher keying rule (ADR-001) and the queued-write conflict rule (ADR-006) match the acceptance criteria they claim to satisfy, and the retention split (ADR-004) does not orphan a foreign key across the academic/financial boundary. The one load-bearing claim that does not survive verification is ADR-002's "corrected" ledger invariant: traced against its own worked overpayment example, the per-enrollment formula is off by exactly `available_credit`, and the per-cycle formula has no term for `credit_apply` even though the same decision explicitly describes generation appending one. This is a Major finding under advisory review — it does not block the design's component shape (append-only entries, derived balances, enrollment-scoped credit are all still the right structure), but the specific equation recorded as "this design satisfies by construction" needs correcting before Functional Design encodes it into the money-module tests the team's practice mandates at 100% branch coverage.
