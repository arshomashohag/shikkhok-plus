| R-02 | Major | performance-requirements.md > Section C, "Pagination" row; scalability-requirements.md > Section C, "Roster pagination" row | Both documents justify "no pagination" from NFR6's ceiling of "100 teachers, a few thousand students" by concluding the largest plausible roster is "tens of rows."
