### Summary

The seven documents are well-reasoned and mostly honest about gaps, but tech-stack-decisions.md B.2 makes a specific, checkable claim of an empirical verification run against a `batch` table that does not exist anywhere in the built tree — the same defect class the brief asked to hunt for, reproduced once more in a document written specifically to correct an earlier instance of it (B.1's TypeDecorator overstatement). The pagination rejection's "tens of rows" ceiling is also presented as derived when it is an unstated distribution assumption. Both are fixable by rewording rather than redesigning; nothing else found rises above Minor.
