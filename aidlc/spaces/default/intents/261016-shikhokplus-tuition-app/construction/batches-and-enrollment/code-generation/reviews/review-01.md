### Summary

The committed code has a genuine, unexercised cross-teacher authorization gap in `remove_enrollment` and `set_inactive_from` — the lens is resolved at the route but silently dropped before reaching the service layer, letting any teacher delete or deactivate another teacher's enrollment by ID. This is the exact "forgotten lens" failure the team's own route-enumeration convention is designed to catch, and it slipped through because that convention checks for a lens *dependency* on the route rather than lens *use* in the handler. Two further issues compound the risk-assessment picture: code-summary.md materially understates what remains unbuilt relative to its own traceability.json (a missing student batch-list route among them), and the `enrollment.batch_id` no-FK decision has a sound rationale for this migration but no recorded forward plan to ever add the constraint. The defect-injection and migration-guard claims I spot-checked (erasure reachability, rollback totality, the two money-gate allowlists' `allOf`/`anyOf` handling) hold up under direct source reading.
