### Summary

Both the Critical IDOR and the two Majors from iteration 1 are genuinely fixed, not just patched at the point the reviewer named: the service layer now has a structural, CI-enforced guarantee (`test_every_write_is_scoped.py`) that no write function can skip lens scoping, which is a stronger answer to "were there three?" than a manual sweep. The route-enumeration gate's root cause (an opaque `_IncludedRouter` wrapper defeating `isinstance`) is fixed at its source and cross-checked against the generated OpenAPI document. The code-summary and FK-forward-plan gaps are both closed with concrete, checkable artifacts rather than restated intentions. The three new findings are all Minor: a disclosed, not hidden, residual weakness in the lens-use gate (already compensated for by the service-side sweep), a mislabeled AC citation that traceability.json itself gets right, and a small stranded client-side string mapping left behind by the scoping fix. None of these block readiness.

I did not run the Android-side JUnit/Turbine suite, `make check` end-to-end, or `mypy`/`ruff` in this pass — turn budget went to reproducing the Critical fix against the pre-fix commit and verifying the gate rewrites directly, which was the highest-value use of it. I also did not spot-check the ~14 other Deferred traceability rows beyond AC2.6.1/AC2.6.2/AC1.3.4/AC2.6.3/AC2.7.1, which the summary now names; I have no reason to suspect them given the demonstrated accuracy of the ones I did check, but they are unverified by me.
