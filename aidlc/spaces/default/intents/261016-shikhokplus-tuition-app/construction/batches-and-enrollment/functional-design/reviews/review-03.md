### Summary

Iteration 2's two findings are genuinely fixed, and the fix for the hardest open question — independent student/guardian erasure against a single relink marker — is correct and goes beyond what was asked, splitting the column per link with a clearly worked rationale. But the same `claimed_at` drift pattern that produced R-08 (a referenced column that was never defined) now appears, live, in BR6.1 and BR6.3 and their corresponding functional-spec.md operations — two rules away from where BR6.2 was just correctly rewritten to stop using it. A developer implementing enrollment removal or notification suppression cannot do so from this document without inventing a column. This is the same defect class, not a new category of problem, which is why the fourth recurrence this reviewer has now seen across B3 and B5 of "fix the finding locally, leave the same error standing nearby" is the one thing still blocking READY.
