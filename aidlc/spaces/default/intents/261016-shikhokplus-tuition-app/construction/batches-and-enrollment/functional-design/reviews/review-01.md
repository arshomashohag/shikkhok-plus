### Summary

The business-rule writing is careful and the money/privacy reasoning (BR3.1, BR4.1's non-proration, BR6.3) is sound in isolation, but the guardian design — the Unit's own stated top risk — is specified against a `GuardianLens` resolution mechanism that the already-built identity-and-access code does not implement: it resolves one guardian enrollment via `role='guardian'`/`LIMIT 1`, not many via `guardian_account_id`. That alone blocks READY. Three further Major gaps (the "narrow the U3 test" instruction has no module boundary to narrow to, the money TypeDecorator BR3.2 names does not exist, and `deleted_at`'s only described case has no write path) compound it past the two-Major threshold.
