### Summary

The two structurally unusual decisions this Unit exists to prove out — the computed lens (BR1.1–BR1.4) and the privacy boundary (BR3.1–BR3.3) — are genuinely mechanical rather than aspirational, and the skeleton-verification problem (empty `Owner`/`Enrollment` tables) is solved correctly by BR6.3's seed script. The blocking issues are narrower: the existence-oracle defense (BR2.7) stops short of the asymmetry that actually matters most (a DB write vs. no write, not just a hash computation), the rate-limit check order is stated two different and partially contradictory ways across the two main documents, and the seed-script mechanism that makes the skeleton checkpoint achievable is undocumented in the operational build spec. These are fixable without rework of the design's structure.
