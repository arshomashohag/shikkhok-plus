### Summary

The linking precedence (BR2.1, BR3.3), pending-only matching and password-bound replacement are sound in shape, but three design claims fail against the built code or against the spec's own rules: the ceiling consume commits mid-transaction, the accepted-link refusal has no row lock, and the email lookup's account half reintroduces an account-existence oracle. Fix those three and decide the administrator-allowlist consequence before this is implementable without guessing.
