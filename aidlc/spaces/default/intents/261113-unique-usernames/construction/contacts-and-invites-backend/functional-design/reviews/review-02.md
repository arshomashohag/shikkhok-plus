### Summary

All seven prior findings are fixed, and the R-03 decision not to consult centre invitations is sound. Two Major concerns remain, within the READY allowance of at most two: the lock order leaves out account rows and foreign-key key-share locks, and the erasure guarantees describe code that does not exist. Both are fixable at code generation but should be corrected in the spec before the unit is built.
