# Code Generation Plan — contacts-and-invites-backend (U2)

## Summary

- Builds: email recording on students, seats and centre invitations; one contact matcher; contact replace and remove; the email form of the already-added lookup; relink markers set at account deletion; a bounded deadlock and serialization retry.
- Touches: migrations/versions/0034_*, app/db/models.py, app/accounts/ (new matching.py; service.py, contact.py), app/students/, app/batches/, app/centres/, app/governance/deletion.py, app/auth/ceilings.py, app/auth/otp.py, app/api/ and app/schemas/ for those routes, app/request_scope.py, app/logging_setup.py, message-keys.json, openapi.json, Makefile.
- Tests: about 60 pytest tests in 9 new or extended files, unit and integration against PostgreSQL 16 as the restricted role; 16 written first.

- Functional design: `construction/contacts-and-invites-backend/functional-design/` — `rules.md` (BR1.1–BR5.13), `entities.md`, `functional-spec.md` (W1–W9, § 1 call sites and readers, § 3 state machines, § 6 error catalogue, § 7 concurrency, § 8 schema).
