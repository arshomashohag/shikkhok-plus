### Summary

The design matches the built backend contract on every endpoint, field, nullability and message key I checked. It correctly finds the live `MemberDto.phone` decode defect and the 15 unmapped keys, and it keeps username rules, the contact-removal decision and availability off the client. It is READY with two Major findings: the gate fails open when the account read fails, and the test table omits the session-flag and HTTP request-shape verifiers. The Minor items are cheap to fold in during code generation.
