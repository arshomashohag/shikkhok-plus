### Summary

I counted the `account.id` references independently and got 15 — eight CASCADE, seven SET NULL — which matches the revised design, and both cascade fixes in `0007` are necessary and correctly reasoned. The classification test in § 2 is sound and survives the two cases it was challenged on: `reminder_event` and `queued_submission` are documented teacher-authored writes, so a student's deletion never reaches them, and there is no third mis-sided column. What the revision does not yet carry is execution risk in `0007` itself: both enrollment constraints are stored under names that differ from the model's convention, because `0002` renamed the column without renaming the constraint, so a migration written against the model's names fails on a migrated database — and no test compares the two. Teacher self-deletion remains the one deletion path nobody has specified.
