- 2026-10-23 (D2) — BR2.2 says a seat with no guardian link reports `absent` rather than `pending`, and the obvious reading — `guardian_account_id IS NULL` — is wrong in the expensive direction. `_LINK_GUARDIAN_SEATS` fills the account id only at signup, so a guardian the teacher recorded who has not signed up yet has a typed number and a NULL account id, and they are genuinely pending: they were asked and have not answered. Fixed the measurement instead of the number, and **the floor rose 35.26 -> 45.84**: removing unmeasurable lines tightens a ratchet rather than loosening it.

Moved to a `canDecline` field the mapper decides, with two mapper tests. Worth recording that the rule is easy to break while writing prose that cites it: the file's own docstring claimed no such conditional existed.
