### Summary

The scope boundary is right and the amendment discipline is good: naming each superseded criterion beside its replacement is the correct call, and extending US1.2's title and narrative was right rather than overreach — the human's answer protected the ID, and a heading reading "claims a dormant record" above criteria saying signing in claims nothing would have been the worse artifact.
