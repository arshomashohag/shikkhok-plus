### Summary

The four artboards exist, match their named locations on the canvas, reuse the design system faithfully, and T7's sixth column is accurate work. What holds this back is that the written specs describe screens slightly better than the screens are: a guardian is told declining stops a fee that AC1.14.1a says keeps running, O4's error state is specified as safety-critical and not drawn, the teacher deletion variant lacks the AC9.4.3 disclosure its own spec says it has, and three accessibility and design-system claims (contrast figures, chip distinguishability, the lens-free invitation screen) do not survive checking against the artboards and FR1.9d.1. The O1 correction is honest and its stated lesson is the right one.
