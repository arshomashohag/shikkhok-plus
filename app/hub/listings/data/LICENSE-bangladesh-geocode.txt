bd_area.csv is derived from nuhil/bangladesh-geocode
(https://github.com/nuhil/bangladesh-geocode), itself generated from the
Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics geocodes. Fetched 2026-11-15 from the
master branch (raw.githubusercontent.com, divisions/divisions.json,
districts/districts.json, upazilas/upazilas.json); the commit hash was not
retrievable in the build environment. Names and bbs_code values are
unmodified; only the format was converted to CSV.

bd_city_area.csv is derived from the same dataset. Fetched 2026-11-17 from
the master branch (raw.githubusercontent.com,
city_corporations/city_corporations.json, postcodes/postcodes.json, and
districts/districts.json to resolve each city corporation's district); the
commit hash was not retrievable in the build environment. City corporation
rows carry the dataset's name, bn_name and bbs_code unmodified. Metropolitan
thana rows are the distinct `thana` values of the postcode rows whose
link_method is `metro_thana`, grouped by city_corporation_id; their English
names are unmodified and their codes ("MT" + the city corporation's
bbs_code digits + a two-digit ordinal in English-name order) are assigned
by this project, not by the dataset.

bd_metro_thana_bn.csv is not derived from the dataset, which carries no
Bangla names for metropolitan thanas: those names were entered by hand for
this project.
