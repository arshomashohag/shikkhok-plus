ShikkhokPlus Public Hub — concept mockups

Six 360dp frames drawn with the product's own tokens (web/src/styles/tokens.css). No new colours: ratings are ink, “Verified” uses primary-container, “Under review” reuses the dashed verifying treatment. Numbers the app generates are Latin; text people typed stays as typed. Photos are placeholders — the hub never shows identifiable children. Aligned to the team's final decisions: guardian-only reviews, month-level dates, no PDFs in V1, contact only when the owner publishes a number. Switch your system theme to see dark mode.

Reading these frames
  • Reviewer identity is a server-composed pseudonym; small batches drop class and tenure. No names, initials or photos of minors anywhere.
  • Averages appear only from 5 reviews (frame a, second card); counts and distribution are always shown.
  • There is no Delete on the owner side — only Reply and Report.
