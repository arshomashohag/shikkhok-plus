For coaching centres

Run the whole centre from one app

Open a centre, invite your managers and teachers by phone number, and build the week's timetable. Managers see and do everything; each teacher sees only their own classes.

  • Managers and teachers

    Invite people by phone number. Nobody gets access until they accept, and someone you remove loses access at once.

  • Timetable and rooms

    Every class with its teacher and room. Change one class or swap two teachers; a teacher or a room booked twice at the same time is refused.

  • Teachers see their own classes

    A centre teacher gets their schedule and does what the class needs — topic, attendance and marks. No fees, no phone numbers, no other teacher's classes.

  • Staff and payroll

    Keep records for teachers and other staff with their pay terms, monthly or per class. Draw each month's salaries, add a bonus or deduction, approve, and record what was paid.

  • Exam fees and other fees

    Charge an exam fee or any other fee beside the monthly fee. Approving a payment records what it was for.

  • Expenses and a monthly summary

    Write down exam paper, printing, rent and the rest, and see each month what came in, what went out and what is still owed.

ShikkhokPlus records money; it never moves it. Fees are paid to you directly, and salaries and expenses are paid outside the app and written down in it.

For individual tutors

Faster than the notebook

Everything a private tutor keeps track of, on the phone you already carry. Your own batches stay entirely yours — even if you also teach at a centre, with the same account.

  • Batches and schedule

    Set up each batch with its weekly schedule. Move or cancel a class and its students and guardians are told.

  • Topic and attendance in seconds

    Record what you covered and who came. Next time, the app suggests where you left off.

  • Exams and marks

    Enter a whole batch's marks without stopping, publish the results, and correct them if you need to.

  • Fees and dues

    Set the monthly fee for each batch, see who owes what across every batch, and send reminders that families see in the app.

  • Verify payments

    When a family reports a bKash, Nagad or bank payment, check the transaction ID, then approve it, approve part of it, or reject it with a note. Cash you are handed is recorded too.

  • Notices

    Post a notice to a batch and every student and guardian in it sees it.

For students and guardians

Classes, results and fees, all in one place

Your teacher adds you by phone number. Sign in with that number, accept the invitation, and everything about your batches is there.

  • Classes and changes

    Your batches, the next class, and any change your teacher makes to the schedule.

  • Results and progress

    The marks from every exam, and a chart of progress test by test.

  • Fees

    What is due, what has been paid, and the full fee history.

  • Send a payment for checking

    Paid by bKash, Nagad, bank or cash? Send the details from the app and your teacher confirms them. A guardian can send one payment for several children.

  • Every child in one account

    A guardian sees each child's batches, results and payments, whichever teacher or centre they study with.

  • Your own details stay yours

    Nothing a student or guardian writes about themselves is shown to any teacher.

How fee payments work

The money goes straight to the teacher

ShikkhokPlus never holds or moves money. It keeps an honest record that the teacher and the family both see.

  1. 1. The family pays

    Directly to the teacher or centre, by bKash, Nagad, Rocket, a bank or cash, as they always have.

  2. 2. They report it

    The amount and the transaction ID go to the teacher from the app, marked as being verified.

  3. 3. The teacher checks

    Approved, approved in part, or rejected with a note — and the dues update for everyone.

Free and private

Built for children's data

Most people using ShikkhokPlus are school students, so privacy is part of how it is built, not an afterthought.

  • Free, with no ads

    Tutors, centres, students and guardians use ShikkhokPlus without paying, and there is no advertising of any kind.

  • No tracking

    No advertising ID, no device identifiers, no location, and no analytics or advertising SDKs — checked automatically on every release.

  • Private by design

    A teacher can never see what a student or guardian writes about themselves; the database itself enforces it. Data is encrypted at rest.

You can delete your account at any time: in the app under More → Delete account, or on the account deletion page without installing anything.
